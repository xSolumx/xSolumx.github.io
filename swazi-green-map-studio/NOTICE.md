# Data, imagery and publication notice

This package contains a detailed planning map of a real site, including precise
coordinates, buildings, nursery areas, water infrastructure and interpreted
routes. Treat the source repository as sensitive. GitHub Pages sites are public
on the internet even when their repository is private, unless a separately
configured enterprise access-control feature says otherwise.

The nine dated image layers were registered for visual comparison. Their labels
refer to acquisition dates, not archive release years. Imagery credits carried
by the editor are Esri / Maxar / Vantor and source imagery contributors.
Elevation context is based on Copernicus DEM GLO-30, 2021 release / EU, ESA, DLR
and Airbus. Those names remain the property of their respective owners. This
repository does not grant additional rights to third-party imagery or data.

The drawn map and illustrated aerial contain interpreted and illustrative
detail. They are not a cadastral, engineering or construction survey. Repeated
plant marks are not species identifications or inventory counts. The mapped
canal confidence classes, irrigation routes and terrain contours retain the
limitations documented in the editor and the repository evidence records.

adds one, normal copyright rules apply to the original code, artwork and
